Common questions

How do I make the letters bigger?
The app follows your iPhone's text size: Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size.

How do I hear the clue or say my answer?
Use Read clue and Speak right above the keyboard. The first time, allow the microphone and speech recognition when asked; you can change this later in the iPhone Settings app.

How do I check if a word is right?
Tap the green Check button under the clue. It is free.

I bought coins or VIP on another device.
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel VIP?
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Big Crossword → Cancel Subscription.
