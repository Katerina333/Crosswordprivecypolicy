Privacy Policy

Big Crossword (WordGrid) for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 27 September 2026

In short: Big Crossword has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not ask you to create an account. Your puzzles, progress and settings stay on your device. We (the developer) do not collect or receive any personal data about you.

Who we are

Big Crossword is published by ITPM GROUP ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your puzzle progress, scores, daily streak, coin balance, player name and avatar, and your settings (such as theme, text and sound options) on your device only. We cannot see this information. It is removed when you delete the app.

Information we do not collect

  • No advertising, advertising identifiers or ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • No account, email address or contact details.
  • No location, contacts or photos.

Services provided by Apple

Some features use Apple's own services. Apple handles that data under the Apple Privacy Policy; we do not receive it.

  • Purchases and VIP subscription are processed by the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only learns which products you own so it can unlock them.
  • Game Center (optional, for challenging friends): if you sign in, Apple shares your Game Center player name and an anonymous player identifier with the app, and the puzzle and results of a challenge are sent to the friend you invite through Apple's servers.
  • Voice: reading clues aloud uses the voice built into your device.
  • Voice: speaking answers (optional) uses the microphone and Apple's speech recognition. It runs on your device whenever your device supports it; otherwise Apple processes the audio to turn it into text. The app does not record or keep any audio. You can turn microphone and speech access off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Daily reminders (optional) are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Other online requests

  • "Learn" hint: when you choose it, the app asks Wikipedia for a short summary of the answer word. Wikipedia receives that word and your device's internet address like any website visit; see the Wikimedia Privacy Policy.
  • New puzzles: the app may download new puzzle files from our content server. These requests contain no personal information; the server, like any web server, briefly sees your device's internet address.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing we hold about you to access, correct or delete. You can erase everything the app stores by deleting the app. If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you still have the rights given by the GDPR, and you may contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
